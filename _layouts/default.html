Aditya Pratap Singh

PhD candidate · Physically consistent generative models

I am a third-year PhD student in Computer Science & Engineering at the University of Michigan, advised by Bernadette Bucher. I work on physically consistent predictive and generative models with applications in robotics and remote sensing.

Previously, I was a Machine Learning Engineer at Visual Concepts Entertainment, building ML solutions for animation, graphics and motion systems in the NBA 2K series.

I have an MSE in Computer & Information Science from the University of Pennsylvania, where I worked with Kostas Daniilidis on vision-and-language navigation. I also hold a B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering from VIT Vellore, India.
